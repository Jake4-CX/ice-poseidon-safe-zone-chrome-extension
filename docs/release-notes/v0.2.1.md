## Fixed
- **Players disappeared during an EMP**, even with *Settings → Blackouts* turned on. The server stops sending all player data for the length of an EMP, so SafeZone+ now keeps showing everyone as they were last seen: on the map with a ⏸ dashed marker, and in the player list with their last known HP. Live updates resume when the EMP ends.
- The "⏸ Name · 3m 12s ago" label on frozen players now counts up every second instead of once a minute.

## New
- **Chests during an EMP.** The server hides every chest during a blackout too. SafeZone+ now shows the unclaimed chests it saw just before, with a dashed yellow ring (some may have been claimed since).
- Last known positions, HP and chests are remembered across page reloads, so reloading during an EMP no longer loses them. SafeZone+ needs to have been open at some point before the EMP.

## Update
Download `safezone-plus-v0.2.1.zip` below, replace the contents of your SafeZone+ folder with it and press ↻ on SafeZone+ in `chrome://extensions`. Your favourites and settings are kept.

New install: unzip it to a folder you'll keep, open `chrome://extensions`, turn on **Developer mode**, click **Load unpacked** and select the folder.
