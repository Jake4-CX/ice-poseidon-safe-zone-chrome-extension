## New
- **No more flashing EMP effect.** During an EMP or smoke blackout the site plays lightning bolts, flashes, scanlines and makes the whole map flicker and warp. SafeZone+ now turns all of that off and shows a calm stand-in instead, so it's still obvious a blackout is on:
  - a soft blue frame around the map,
  - **⚡ EMP blackout · ends in 4:07** at the top of the map, counting down,
  - the same countdown in the SafeZone+ panel header, so you see it even with the panel collapsed.

  The site's own "An EMP has blacked out the map until …" notice stays too. Prefer the original effect? Untick **Settings → Blackouts → Turn off the flashing EMP effect**.

## Fixed
- The panel header showed **0 alive** if you opened the map during an EMP. It now counts the same way as the Alive filter.

## Update
Download `safezone-plus-v0.3.0.zip` below, replace the contents of your SafeZone+ folder with it and press ↻ on SafeZone+ in `chrome://extensions`. Your favourites and settings are kept.

New install: unzip it to a folder you'll keep, open `chrome://extensions`, turn on **Developer mode**, click **Load unpacked** and select the folder.
